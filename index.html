Creanotes

Script and note-taking app built for content creators

Available on Microsoft Store

My first and most ambitious app, built in under a month. It focuses on what content creators actually need: gathering and managing media, a script-writing workflow, and much more. Built with opencode.

03

Perpul

Know what you can afford before you buy

Available on GitHub

Perpul turns your balance and payday into a daily spending allowance, then tells you whether a planned purchase still fits your budget before you buy. It shows the new allowance and the percentage drop first. A savings simulator tests 10, 20, or 30 percent targets and projects your savings up to three years.

Flutter / Dart Android Offline-first Daily allowance Savings pot Indonesian / Rupiah
